Open source, en français et en anglais.

Sillon demande ton propre serveur Plex ou Jellyfin. C'est un lecteur indépendant, sans lien avec Plex Inc., le projet Jellyfin ou Sonos.

## English (en-US)

**App name**
```
Sillon — Music Player
```

**Short description** (≤80)
```
Your Plex or Jellyfin music, no ads, no tracking. Android Auto and offline.
```

**Full description** (≤4000)
```
Your music lives on your server. Sillon makes it a pleasure to play on your phone, in the car and on the go: no ads, no tracking, no subscription.

Pick Plex or Jellyfin, sign in, play. No account but your server's.

MADE FOR MUSIC
• Albums, artists, playlists and search, gapless playback
• Big covers and a mini-player always within reach
• Background playback with lock-screen, notification, Bluetooth and headset controls

RADIOS AND DISCOVERY
• Your library radios, a radio for every artist, and from the player a radio of tracks that sound like the one playing
• Never played: a radio of the tracks you have never heard, or an unplayed album drawn at random
• Mystery sleeve: a whole album you rarely play, hidden behind a kraft sleeve until the third track

YOUR TASTE, FRONT AND CENTRE
• Star ratings on every track, popularity bars to spot an album's hits
• My 5 stars: one tap shuffles every track you love
• Sleeve wear: covers age as you play them, like a real record

IN THE CAR
• Android Auto: browse your library with its covers, resume where you stopped, or say "play … on Sillon"

OFFLINE AND DATA
• Download albums and playlists, Wi-Fi only by default
• Original quality on Wi-Fi, AAC at 320, 192 or 128 kbps on mobile data, transcoded by your server
• Even volume with the ReplayGain levels your server measured

AND MORE
• Listen together: several phones play the same track at the same moment, through your own server (Jellyfin)
• Play on Sonos, over your local network, no account and no cloud
• Music postcards to share: cover, title, artist and year, drawn on your phone
• Your server stays in sync: what you play shows in its history

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No analytics, no ads, no Sillon account. Your server token never leaves the app.

Open source, in English and French.

Sillon requires your own Plex or Jellyfin server. It is an independent player, not affiliated with Plex Inc., the Jellyfin project or Sonos.
```

## App access (for Google reviewers)

Sillon needs a media server. Reviewers can use the public Jellyfin demo:

- Choose **Jellyfin** on the first screen
- Server address: `https://demo.jellyfin.org/stable`
- User name: `demo`, password: leave empty
